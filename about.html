Our Story

Rizal Park, also known as Luneta Park, is one of the most famous historical parks in the Philippines. It is located in Manila and is dedicated to Dr. Jose Rizal, the national hero of the country.

Why visit rizal park?

  • Learn about Philippine history
  • Enjoy the beautiful scenery
  • Relax with family and friends
